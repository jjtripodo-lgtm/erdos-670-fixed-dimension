Those companions were released in [public PR #19](https://github.com/jjtripodo-lgtm/erdos-670-fixed-dimension/pull/19); they should not be described as still private.

## Reproducibility: what this update actually supplies

Run `python3 research_updates/verify_finite_window_structure.py` at the repository root. This small **standard-library-only structural verifier** checks:

* Each specified finite witness prime and the exact integer sizes obtained from `n=6k-r`.
* Complete coverage of `253..294` and `294..318`, including the B48/B49 seams and the overlap with the already source-written analytic tail.
* The exact rational robust normalization margin `(2000000/2095999)*(131/125)-1-4/10000000 = 404001/5239997500000 > 0`.
* That 288 vertices have exactly 41,328 unordered distinct edges.

This is **not** an independent verifier of the entire geometry, all 43,547 + 31,220 finite local gap inequalities, Gram/Schur positivity over real boxes, the inherited all-size analytic step, the provenance of the original 29-dimensional model, or any Lean proof. A passing run must never be cited as proof of the complete `n>=253` theorem. The underlying new proof scripts are not bundled here because their historical transitive dependencies have not yet been exported, licensed, and rerun as one complete public package.
