3. **Boon Suan Ho.** “Erdős's diameter conjecture for separated distances fails in high dimensions.” arXiv:2604.15305v1, 16 April 2026. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15305v1), [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15305v1), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.15305v1). All six pages were read earlier and full HTML freshly reread. Relevant locations: Theorem 1, Remark 9, Remark 10. Growing dimension is explicit; the 0.885589… coefficient is proved asymptotically, while 0.85411 is numerical. Its reported Lean formalization was not replayed here.

4. **P. Erdős and P. Turán.** “On a Problem of Sidon in Additive Number Theory, and on some Related Problems.” *Journal of the London Mathematical Society* **16** (1941), 212–215. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s1-16.4.212), [institutional original scan](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1941-01.pdf). It supplies a classical quadratic-residue uniqueness ingredient, not a fixed-dimensional geometric counterexample. The scan is not bundled here.
